Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

"Don't Touch My Phone" ("the app") is developed by Unique Fifties. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

Summary

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind, and makes no network requests.

Microphone access

The app requests microphone access only so you can record your own custom guard alarm sound. Any recording you make is saved solely on your device's private app storage and is never uploaded, shared, or transmitted anywhere. You can delete it at any time from within the app.

Notifications

The app requests notification permission to show an ongoing status notification while guard mode is active. This notification is generated and shown entirely on your device.

Data sharing

The app does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to begin with.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to unique.fifties@gmail.com.
